About Ardas Social Kitchen

OUR STORY

Founded by Ashni Kumar and Priya Trivedi, former owners of the beloved Guru’s Indian Cuisine in Newtown, Ardas Social Kitchen brings authentic Indian cuisine and a powerful spirit of giving back to the Bucks County community.

Our daily lunch service features a homestyle vegetarian buffet offered on a pay-what-you-wish basis, making wholesome meals accessible to everyone. Dinner includes many of the signature dishes that made Guru’s a local favorite, along with Indian street food, curries, biryanis, tandoori specialties, vegetarian selections, and complete Thali meals available for dine-in or takeout.

Giving back has always been central to Ashni and Priya’s mission. Through their nonprofit, Guru’s Guardian Angels, they help rescue, rehabilitate, and care for animals in need while also providing complimentary meals to local families experiencing hardship.

Ardas Social Kitchen is more than a restaurant—it is a welcoming community gathering place where authentic food, compassion, and purpose come together to support both people and animals.
